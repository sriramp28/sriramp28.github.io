Geophysics & Oceanography Research

Mixing, Memory, and Observation in Stratified Waters.

I study how finite-depth interfaces, double-diffusive instabilities, inherited spectral structure, and mean shear organize the movement of heat and salt. High-resolution simulations are paired with sparse sampling and hydrographic observations to connect resolved transport pathways with what can be measured in the ocean.

Geophysical and environmental fluid dynamics

One interface problem, five complementary views.

The current program moves from route selection in resolved salt-finger fields to finite-interface memory, sparse observability, mean-shear survival, and polar hydrographic structure. Together, the studies ask what an interface remembers, how that memory changes exchange, and what environmental measurements can recover.
