Research

Transport Across Aerospace and Geophysical Flows.

My research examines how interfaces, coherent structures, and instabilities regulate heat, salt, momentum, and reactive material. High-resolution simulation, mathematical diagnostics, and measurement-aware analysis connect local fluid mechanics to propulsion systems, reacting shear layers, stratified oceans, and polar interfaces.

Aerospace and engineered fluid dynamics Propulsion, reacting flows, acoustics, turbulence, numerical modeling, and design
02
Geophysical and environmental fluid dynamics Ocean mixing, polar interfaces, stratification, sparse observation, and finite-depth transport

Two research programs

Distinct systems, shared questions.

Aerospace and geophysical flows differ in scale, forcing, and purpose. I approach both through the same physical questions about how interfaces, coherent structures, and instabilities organize transport, how numerical resolution changes what we infer, and what practical measurements can recover.

Aerospace Engineering

Reacting flows, propulsion, acoustics, and computation.

This program examines mixing, chemical heat release, compressibility, combustion noise, coherent structure, entropy transport, and simulation-guided engineering design.

Explore aerospace research

Geophysics & Oceanography

Stratification, ocean mixing, and environmental observation.

This program studies finite-depth double diffusion, interfacial memory, route survival, polar heat transport, and what sparse temperature and salinity measurements preserve.
